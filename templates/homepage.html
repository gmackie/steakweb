omniDECT gives your cordless handset a number on the camp network. To self-install:

  1. Register a DECT extension below. You'll get an activation code (tap Get Credentials to see it).
  2. Pair your GAP handset to the nearest omniDECT base.
  3. On the handset, dial *77 and enter your activation code. The base binds your handset and reads back your extension.

For help or non-standard service, please see the omniDECT service desk.
